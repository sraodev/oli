# Code of Conduct

Oli welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
Be respectful, constructive and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people;
accept corrections and make room for newcomers.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, publishing private
information and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. These rules
apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces where
someone represents the project.

Report concerns privately to [srao.dev@gmail.com](mailto:srao.dev@gmail.com).
Share only information needed to understand the incident. Maintainers will
handle reports as privately as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed when responding to threats or legal obligations.

Maintainers may remove content, request a correction, warn participants or
temporarily/permanently restrict participation according to severity and
repeated behavior. If you believe a decision was mistaken, send relevant
context through the same private channel and request reconsideration.

This is a project-specific policy, not a promise of professional moderation
or a particular response time.
